# Lead Outreach Strategy — Global

## Section 1 — Ideal Client Profile (ICP)

Only pursue leads that match this profile. Time spent on bad-fit leads is time stolen from good ones.

**ICP criteria:**

| Criteria | Ideal | Acceptable | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Business size | 1-20 employees | 20-100 employees | Enterprise (100+) |
| Monthly website visitors | 500-10,000 | 100-500 | Under 100 |
| Current support method | Email only / WhatsApp | Basic live chat | Dedicated support team |
| Technical ability | Non-technical owner | Has a developer | Large IT dept |
| Budget signal | Pays for other SaaS tools | Open to monthly fees | Purely free-tool user |
| Language | English primary | Bilingual | Non-English only |
| Geography | US, UK, Canada, Australia, Nigeria | Europe, India, SE Asia | N/A |

**Perfect client:** A small ecommerce store or SaaS product in an English-speaking market, run by a non-technical founder, currently handling support via email or WhatsApp, already paying for tools like Shopify, Mailchimp, or Notion.

**Pain points** — what they feel before finding SupportBot:

| Pain | What They Say | How SupportBot Fixes It |
|---|---|---|
| Drowning in repetitive questions | "I answer the same 10 emails every day" | Auto-replies handle 80% instantly |
| Support costs too much | "I can't afford a support person" | Bot costs less than 1 hour of staff time/month |
| Slow response times | "Customers complain we don't reply fast" | Instant 24/7 responses |
| Missing leads after hours | "People visit at night and we lose them" | Lead capture runs while they sleep |
| Can't scale support | "Adding clients breaks our support" | Bot scales infinitely |

## Section 2 — Target Industries (Global)

Ranked by conversion likelihood. Start with Tier 1. Move to Tier 2 only after landing 3 Tier 1 clients.

### Tier 1 — Highest Conversion Likelihood

#### 1. Ecommerce stores (Shopify, WooCommerce)

**Why they need SupportBot:** Online stores get the same five product questions on repeat — sizing, shipping, returns, payment, stock. Every unanswered question after-hours is a cart that doesn't convert.

- **Where to find them:** Shopify app store reviews, ProductHunt, IndieHackers, Etsy sellers who also have a Shopify/Woo storefront.
- **Angle:** "You're losing sales to unanswered questions at 2am."
- **Hook:** "I checked your store — your contact page is just an email form. You're losing 24-hour buyers."

#### 2. SaaS products (small / indie)

**Why they need SupportBot:** Trial users hit a friction point, can't get help in the next 30 minutes, and never come back. Indie founders can't be on Intercom 24/7 — but their churn rate behaves as if they should be.

- **Where to find them:** ProductHunt, IndieHackers, Twitter/X `#buildinpublic`, Micro-SaaS directories.
- **Angle:** "Your trial users churn because support is too slow."
- **Hook:** "Your free trial is 14 days but your support response time is 2. Want to fix the math?"

#### 3. Digital agencies (web design / marketing)

**Why they need SupportBot:** Agencies live and die by recurring revenue. White-labelling SupportBot lets them add a chatbot line item to every client retainer with zero engineering load.

- **Where to find them:** Clutch.co, DesignRush, LinkedIn, local Facebook groups.
- **Angle:** "Resell SupportBot to your clients — white label ready."
- **Hook:** "You build sites. I built the chatbot for those sites — you keep the margin."

> Agencies are a **CHANNEL**, not just a client. One agency can bring 5-10 sub-clients. Treat agency leads as 10x the value of a single direct client.

#### 4. Online course creators / coaches

**Why they need SupportBot:** Course creators get the same enrollment, refund, and "how do I access my course" questions every day. A bot trained on the course FAQ frees them to focus on creating new content.

- **Where to find them:** Gumroad, Teachable, Kajabi community, Twitter/X, LinkedIn.
- **Angle:** "Your students ask the same questions — automate answers."
- **Hook:** "How many times this week did someone ask you 'how do I log in to the course'? Let's give that question to a bot."

#### 5. Real estate agents / property listings

**Why they need SupportBot:** Property buyers browse at night. By the time the agent sees the email the next morning, the lead has emailed three other agents. Speed of first response is the entire game.

- **Where to find them:** Zillow profiles, Realtor.com, LinkedIn, local real estate Facebook groups.
- **Angle:** "Capture leads from your listings at any hour."
- **Hook:** "A buyer scrolls your listing at 11pm. You see it at 9am. By then they've moved on. Want to fix that?"

### Tier 2 — Good Fit, Longer Sales Cycle

6. **Restaurants with online ordering / reservations** — booking and menu queries are perfectly repetitive. But owner attention is split between operations and the front of house, so demos must be quick. Find via Google Maps "X cuisine near Y" + their website.
7. **Healthcare clinics (appointment booking queries)** — high need but compliance hesitancy. Lead with "appointment booking only, no medical advice." Find via local clinic directories.
8. **Recruitment agencies** — heavy inbound from candidates asking about job status. Find on LinkedIn + recruitment-focused Facebook groups.
9. **Financial advisors / accountants** — strong fit for client portal questions ("when is my report ready?"). Compliance-shy on advice; lead with "FAQ-only" framing. Find via LinkedIn.
10. **Travel agencies / tour operators** — big spike of repetitive pre-booking questions; quiet rest of the year. Pricing must reflect seasonality. Find via Google Maps + niche travel forums.

### Tier 3 — Opportunistic Only

These industries have need but longer decision cycles, more stakeholders, or compliance concerns. Pursue only if **inbound** — do not cold outreach here. Includes: Banks, hospitals, law firms, government.

## Section 3 — Outreach Channels

Use channels in this priority order. Master one before adding the next.

**Channel priority:**

| Priority | Channel | Effort | Speed to Result | Best For |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cold email | Low | 1-2 weeks | Ecommerce, SaaS |
| 2 | LinkedIn DM | Medium | 1-3 weeks | Agencies, B2B SaaS |
| 3 | Twitter/X DM | Low | Days | Indie founders, `#buildinpublic` |
| 4 | Facebook Groups | Medium | 1-2 weeks | Local businesses, coaches |
| 5 | ProductHunt | Low | Days | SaaS, tools |
| 6 | IndieHackers | Low | Days | Indie founders |
| 7 | Upwork | Medium | 1-2 weeks | One-off installs → convert to retainer |
| 8 | Referrals | Low | Immediate | Existing clients → their network |

**Channel notes:**

- Start with cold email + Twitter/X DM simultaneously.
- LinkedIn is slower but higher ticket value.
- **Upwork:** list as "AI Chatbot Setup Service" — one-off project converts to monthly maintenance.
- **Referrals** activate after the first 3 clients — offer one free month for a successful referral.

## Section 4 — Outreach Message Templates

These are starting points — personalise every message. A personalised bad message beats a perfect template every time.

### Template 1 — Cold Email (Ecommerce)

**Subject lines (A/B test):**

- A: `Quick question about [Store Name]'s support`
- B: `Your customers are asking questions at 2am`
- C: `I checked [Store Name] — here's what I found`

**Body:**

```
Hi [First Name],

I was browsing [Store Name] and noticed you're handling
support manually — [specific observation e.g. "I see your
contact page is just an email form"].

I built SupportBot Studio — an AI chatbot that sits on your
website and instantly answers your customers' questions 24/7,
captures leads, and alerts you only when a human is needed.

Setup takes one afternoon. Monthly cost is less than
a few hours of staff time.

Would it make sense to show you a 10-minute demo?

[Your Name]
SupportBot Studio
```

**Personalisation tips:**

- Replace `[specific observation]` with something real you noticed on their site — broken support link, slow response on chat, etc.
- Mention their product category if you can.
- Never send the same email twice — tools like Hunter.io show if emails are opened.

### Template 2 — Cold Email (SaaS / Indie Founder)

**Subject lines:**

- A: `Your trial users are churning — I might know why`
- B: `SaaS support question for [Product Name]`

**Body:**

```
Hi [First Name],

Saw [Product Name] on ProductHunt — congrats on the launch.

One thing I see kill SaaS trials is slow support response.
Users hit a wall, don't get help fast enough, and churn
before they see the value.

I built SupportBot Studio — installs on your site in one line
of code, answers common questions instantly using your docs,
and escalates complex issues to you via Slack or Telegram.

Happy to set it up for [Product Name] and show you
what it looks like in 10 minutes.

[Your Name]
```

### Template 3 — LinkedIn DM (Agencies)

Keep under 5 lines — LinkedIn DMs get skimmed.

```
Hi [First Name] — I saw [Agency Name] does web projects
for [industry/type of clients].

I built a white-label AI chatbot platform you could offer
your clients — installs in one line, branded to their business,
you set the price.

Worth a 10-min chat?
```

### Template 4 — Twitter/X DM (Indie Founders)

Keep under 3 lines — Twitter DMs are casual.

```
Hey — saw your #buildinpublic posts. Built something
that might help with support: AI chatbot, one line install,
webhook ready. Happy to show you if interested.
```

### Template 5 — Facebook Group Post

For groups like "Shopify Entrepreneurs" or "Online Business Owners":

```
Question for store owners here 👋

How are you currently handling customer support?
Email? WhatsApp? Live chat?

I built an AI chatbot that answers questions instantly,
captures leads after hours, and only pings you when
something needs a human. Works on any website.

Not selling — genuinely curious what people use and
happy to show anyone what it looks like if interested.
[Your Name]
```

⚠️ Don't post links immediately — get replies first. Drop the link only when someone asks. Most groups auto-flag link-led posts as spam and you'll be banned.

### Template 6 — Follow-Up Email (3 days after no reply)

```
Hi [First Name],

Just following up on my note from [day].

No worries if it's not a fit — but if customer support
is something you're thinking about, I'm happy to do
a quick demo with no obligation.

[Your Name]
```

⚠️ Send a maximum of 2 follow-ups per prospect. After 2 no-replies, mark as **Cold** and move on. More follow-ups burn the address and your reputation.

## Section 5 — Demo Script

Use this when a prospect agrees to a demo. Keep it under 15 minutes. Talk less, show more.

**Demo flow:**

| Minute | What to Do | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| 0-2 | Ask: "What does your support look like today?" | Understand their pain |
| 2-5 | Show the widget live on a demo site | Make it real |
| 5-8 | Trigger a FAQ auto-reply | Show instant value |
| 8-10 | Trigger an escalation | Show they stay in control |
| 10-12 | Show admin dashboard — analytics + knowledge base | Show simplicity |
| 12-14 | Show embed code — one line | Remove technical fear |
| 14-15 | Ask: "Does this solve what you described?" | Qualify close |

**Objection handling:**

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "We already have live chat" | "Live chat needs someone online. This works 24/7 with no staff." |
| "It's too expensive" | "What does one hour of your time cost? This costs less per month." |
| "We're too small" | "Small teams benefit most — you get support capacity without hiring." |
| "We need to think about it" | "What would make it an easy yes? Is it the price, the setup, or something else?" |
| "Can it integrate with X?" | "It fires webhooks — connects to anything via Activepieces or Zapier." |
| "What if the AI says something wrong?" | "You control all the answers in the knowledge base. It only says what you tell it." |

**Close line** to use after the demo:

> "If you're happy with what you saw, I can have this live on your site by [specific day]. Setup fee is [X], then [Y] per month. Want to move forward?"

**Targets:**

## Section 8 — Pricing Strategy

Use this pricing structure. Don't undersell. A too-cheap price signals low quality.

| Plan | Setup Fee | Monthly Fee | What's Included | Best For |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | $200 | $100 | 1 bot, 500 msgs/month, email escalation, basic analytics | Solo businesses, early stage |
| **Growth** | $350 | $200 | 1 bot, 2000 msgs/month, Telegram + email, webhooks, lead capture | Growing ecommerce / SaaS |
| **Pro** | $500 | $300 | 1 bot, unlimited msgs, all features, Brand Voice DNA, priority support | Established businesses |
| **Agency** | $800 | $500 | Up to 5 bots, all features, white-label, resell rights | Digital agencies |

**Discount rules:**

- ⚠️ **Never discount the setup fee** — it covers your build time and a discounted setup teaches the client that your time is negotiable.
- Monthly fee: max 20% discount for **annual prepay only**.
- ⚠️ **Never give free months upfront** — offer a free month after the 3rd consecutive payment instead (rewards loyalty, not negotiation).
- If they say "too expensive" — move them down one **plan**, not down in **price** on the same plan.
