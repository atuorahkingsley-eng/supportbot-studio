Small business owners are drowning. Every day, the same five questions hit your inbox: shipping, returns, sizing, hours, pricing. Email piles up. Customers expect instant answers. You can't be online 24/7, and you can't justify $300/month for an enterprise support tool.

**SupportBot Studio** is what fixes that. One line of code on any website. The AI bot reads your FAQs and answers customer questions instantly. It captures leads while you sleep. It escalates to you on **Slack**, **Telegram**, or email only when a human is actually needed. No enterprise contract. No engineering team. Live on your site by the end of the afternoon.

I'm Kay — solo developer from Lagos, Nigeria. I built **SupportBot Studio** because the chatbot tools that exist are either too expensive (Intercom, Drift), too rigid (template-based bots), or too technical (build-your-own frameworks). I wanted something a non-technical founder could install in one afternoon and trust to handle support that night. So I built it.

What makes it different:

- **Brand Voice DNA** — the bot replies in your tone, not robot-corporate.

- **White-label** — agencies get full resell rights and a multi-bot dashboard.
- **Multi-language** — auto-detects visitor language and replies in it.

If you're a small business owner who's tired of repeating yourself to customers, or an agency who wants a chatbot you can resell, this is for you. Click through, kick the tires on the live demo, and DM me on **ProductHunt** if you want a 10-minute walk-through. The launch runs for 24 hours — I'm replying to every comment.

## Section 6 — Comment Response Templates

Respond to every comment within 1 hour on launch day. These templates are starting points — personalise each one.

**1. "This is cool, congrats on the launch"**

> Thanks so much 🙏 means a lot — solo launch days are nerve-wracking. If you have any thoughts on what would make it more useful, I'm all ears. And if you ever try the demo, ping me — happy to walk you through it personally.

**2. "How is this different from Intercom/Drift?"**

> Great question. **Intercom** and **Drift** are built for sales/support teams with budget and a CSM. **SupportBot Studio** is built for small business owners who can't justify $300+/month and don't want a six-week onboarding. One line of code, sensible defaults, monthly fee under $300. If you want enterprise features, Intercom wins. If you want it live by Friday, this might fit. Happy to show the diff in a 10-min demo — DM me.

**3. "What's the pricing?"**

> Setup fee + monthly maintenance. Plans run from $200 setup / $100/month (Starter) up to $800 setup / $500/month (Agency, 5 bots, white-label). All plans include the same core AI — bigger plans add message volume and channels. Happy to send the full pricing breakdown if useful — DM me.

**4. "Does it work with Shopify / WordPress / Webflow?"**

> Yes — works with any platform that lets you paste a `<script>` tag. **Shopify**, **WordPress**, **Webflow**, **Wix**, **Squarespace**, plain HTML — all good. The widget is platform-agnostic. The only thing the host site needs is the ability to add one line before `</body>`. Demo if useful.

**5. "Can I self-host this?"**

> Not yet — currently it's a managed multi-tenant deployment. Self-hosting is on the roadmap for clients with compliance requirements. If self-hosting is a hard requirement for you, DM me and I'll add you to the list — could move that up if there's enough demand.

**6. "How does the AI know what to say?"**

> You upload your FAQs, support docs, or a CSV/PDF — the bot uses those as its knowledge base. It only answers from what you've given it. If a question is outside that, it escalates to you (Slack, Telegram, email). Nothing made up, nothing hallucinated. You stay in control of every answer. Happy to demo on your real FAQs if you want.

**7. "I'm an agency — can I white-label this?"**

> Yes — it's literally one of the plans. Agency tier includes up to 5 bots, all features, full white-label, and resell rights. You set the price you charge your clients, I just power the engine. A handful of agencies already in conversation — DM me if you want to see how the multi-bot dashboard works.

**8. "What happens when the AI doesn't know the answer?"**

> It escalates. You get pinged on **Slack**, **Telegram**, or email (your choice) with the conversation context. The bot also fires an `escalation_triggered` webhook so you can route it to your CRM or ticketing system via **Activepieces**/**Zapier**/**Make**. The visitor sees a graceful "a human will be with you shortly" message — never a hallucination.

**9. "Is there a free trial?"**

> No free trial right now — but I do free 1:1 demos so you can see it solving your specific support problem before paying anything. If you want, drop your URL in a DM and I'll record a 5-minute **Loom** showing the bot answering your actual FAQs. That usually beats a self-serve trial.

**10. "How long does setup take?"**

> From signed agreement to widget live: usually one afternoon. Most of that is collecting your FAQs and brand color. Once those are in, the actual technical install is the script tag — under 5 minutes. I do the configuration end myself for every client so you don't touch the dashboard until it's ready to use.

## Section 10 — If Launch Underperforms

**Underperform** = under 50 upvotes or not in the top 20 by 6pm PST. If this happens, do not panic. Run this recovery plan.

1. Post an honest update comment on PH — "Still early, happy to answer any questions."
2. Double outreach volume on **Twitter/X** and **LinkedIn**.
3. DM 10 more people directly asking for support.
4. Reframe: PH is a marathon not a sprint — products get discovered for weeks after launch day.
5. Use the PH listing as social proof regardless of rank — "As seen on **ProductHunt**" is still valuable.

**Mindset note:**

> A top 10 finish is great. A top 50 finish still builds credibility. Any finish gives you a PH badge, a backlink, and a reason to reach out to prospects. The launch is not the end — it is the beginning of outreach.

⚠️ **Do not delete or unpublish a low-performing launch.** The PH page becomes a permanent backlink and credibility asset. Even a #38-of-the-day finish still reads as "featured on ProductHunt" forever.
