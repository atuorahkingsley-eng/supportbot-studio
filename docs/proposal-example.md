## What We Need From You

To start work, we'll need the following — most clients send this within 1–2 days:

1. **Business basics** — ShopEase legal entity name, one-line description, top 3 product categories
2. **Branding assets** — logo (SVG or high-res PNG), primary brand color (hex), tone preference (friendly / professional / formal)
3. **FAQs** — your 10–30 most common customer questions with answers, or your existing help center URL
4. **Escalation contacts** — email and Telegram username for the team member who handles cases the AI hands off
5. **Website access** — the ability to add a single line of code to shopease.com, either via your developer, your CMS admin, or a 15-minute screenshare with us

If FAQs aren't documented yet, we'll run a short discovery call and write them ourselves — at no extra cost.
